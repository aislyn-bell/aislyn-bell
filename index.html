About Me

Hello! I am a second year PhD student in Physics and Astronomy at Johns Hopkins University. I work with Professor Meredith MacGregor on all things planet formation, with a particular focus on debris disks and exocomets. I graduated from CU Boulder with a degree in Astronomy and a minor in Political Science in 2024, and took a gap year to teach middle and high school students about the beauty of our universe. When I'm not doing science, you can find me enjoying music or travelling!
