Teaching & Outreach

Teaching

I took a gap year before starting my PhD to work as an Astrophysics Educator at the Wings Over the Rockies Air and Space Museum's program for middle and high school students. Once a week through the academic year, students would supplement their typical schoolwork with hands-on aerosapce and astrophysics classes in the 'Wings Aerospace Exploration Program'. As an instructor in this program, I developed curriculum for and taught 7 astronomy courses: Introduction to Astronomy, Humans in Space, Life in the Universe, Black Holes, Space Farming, Science Communication, and Introduction to Python for Astronomy. In addition to my teaching responsibilites, I helped launch a portable planetarium program at the museum to take to local K-12 schools. I created 2 pilot shows for this program: a 'Tour of the Solar System' and 'Missions to Mars'.
